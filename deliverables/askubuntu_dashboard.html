How did question activity change over the MongoDB aggregation years?

Question volume peaked at 40,931 in 2016, then declined to 3,601 in 2024. The 2024 value is a partial year in the April 2024 snapshot.

Which year had the most questions?

2016 had the largest yearly question count: 40,931. Its mean question score was 2.6883 and the aggregation reported 49,321 answers.

Which tags appear most often?

The all-post tag count ranks boot (24,058), networking (22,985), and command-line (22,509) highest. In the MongoDB question-only aggregation, boot also leads with 18,362 questions.

How many questions were answered?

The MongoDB answer-status aggregation reports 184,865 answered questions and 83,677 unanswered questions in the aggregation scope.

What did Hive show for TextFile versus RCFile?

Both tables returned 10 yearly rows with matching year labels. Logical storage was 8,494,848 bytes for TextFile and 7,239,381 bytes for RCFile. In the latest rerun, the end-to-end time was 47.351 s for TextFile and 48.396 s for RCFile. Timing includes starting Beeline and returning query output; it is a single-node benchmark.

Final analysis

What the supplied results support.

The Stack Exchange dataset contains 945,111 posts, including 414,451 questions and 523,728 answers. Question activity is highest around 2015–2016 and trends downward afterward, with 2024 incomplete in the snapshot. Boot, networking, and command-line are the most frequent tags by all-post count.

The project demonstrates document ingestion and indexing in MongoDB, HDFS file operations, a YARN MapReduce aggregation, Pig transformation, and equivalent Hive TextFile/RCFile queries. HDFS/YARN evidence records two live nodes: master 172.16.6.150 and worker1 172.16.4.91.

Data note: the April 2024 archive is a selected Stack Exchange community snapshot, not the full multi-community corpus. Frame/aggregation years reflect record creation years and may not represent a complete calendar year.
